# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General-public consumers in the middle of a high-stakes real estate decision: people
selling or buying a home who want to understand their options and save money on
commissions and fees. They are not industry insiders. They arrive cautious, often
comparing alternatives, and weighing a transaction worth tens or hundreds of thousands
of dollars. Their context is research and evaluation: they need to quickly grasp what
the platform does, why it is credible, and what it will cost them.

## Product Purpose

A real estate / property platform landing page. Its job is to explain the offering
clearly, establish credibility fast, and move a hesitant visitor toward action (list a
home, start a search, get a valuation). Success is a visitor who understands the value,
trusts the brand enough to proceed, and takes the primary action without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy and confident. Three words: credible, clear, reassuring. The voice is
plain-spoken and direct, never hype. It states facts (real dollar figures, real
numbers) rather than adjectives. It guides rather than pressures. The emotional goal is
to make a nervous, money-conscious consumer feel they are in capable, honest hands.

## Anti-references

- The generic Zillow-clone homepage: dense listing-grid above the fold, map-first,
  feature soup.
- Stock "happy family standing in front of a house" / "couple holding keys" photography.
- Pushy discount-coupon sales energy: blaring percentages, countdown urgency, loud
  promo banners.
- Dated traditional-realty aesthetics: agent headshots, gold-and-navy "luxury" cliches,
  cursive script logos.

## Design Principles

- **Make savings concrete.** Show real dollar figures and real numbers, not vague
  "save more" claims. Specificity is what earns belief in a money decision.
- **Earn trust with proof, not adjectives.** Credibility markers (volume listed,
  verified reviews, press, ratings) over self-description.
- **Clarity over jargon.** Real estate is intimidating; use plain language and explain
  the process so a first-timer feels oriented.
- **Confidence without pressure.** Guide the visitor and remove friction; never resort
  to hard-sell urgency tactics that undercut trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. The audience spans a wide age range, including older home sellers,
so prioritize legible type, strong contrast (body text >=4.5:1), and large, obvious
touch targets. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support; never gate content on motion.
Color is never the sole carrier of meaning.
